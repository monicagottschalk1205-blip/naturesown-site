Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer vegan, gluten-free, or other specialty items?

Absolutely! We have dedicated sections for various dietary requirements. Our gluten-free section includes everything from bread and pasta to snacks and baking ingredients, all certified gluten-free. We also carry extensive vegan and plant-based options, including meat alternatives, dairy-free products, and specialty ingredients. For keto and low-carb diets, we stock sugar-free products, healthy fats, and low-carb alternatives. Our staff can help you locate specific items or suggest suitable alternatives for your dietary needs.

Can I return products if I'm not satisfied with them?

We no longer refund or exchange open or consumed items from these categories, even with receipts:

  • Vitamins & Supplements
  • Health & Body Care Products
  • Non-Food Items

Please contact the manufacturer with any questions or concerns.

All other returns must have a receipt.

There are no refunds or exchanges on marked down items.

Where do your fresh produce and meats come from?

We work directly with trusted farmers and suppliers, many of whom are located right here in the Midwest. Our focus is on sourcing sustainably grown produce, responsibly raised meats, and seasonal items whenever possible. By doing this, we ensure you’re getting fresher food that supports local agriculture and reduces environmental impact.

Do you help customers new to natural and organic products?

Absolutely! We know that switching to organic or natural products can feel overwhelming at first. Our team is here to answer questions, suggest alternatives, and help you explore healthier options that fit your lifestyle and budget. Whether you’re curious about clean ingredients, eco-friendly swaps, or specialty diets, we’ll guide you every step of the way.

Can I bring my pet with me when I shop?

Yes! Nature’s Own is a pet-friendly store. We love seeing our customers shop with their furry friends, as long as pets are well-behaved and on a leash.
